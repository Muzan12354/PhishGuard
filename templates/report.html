⚖️ IT ACT, 2000

Know the Law

Section 43

Unauthorized access, damage, or other unauthorized acts involving computer systems.

Section 66

Dishonest or fraudulent computer-related acts covered under Section 43.

Section 66C

Identity theft — fraudulent use of another person's password, electronic signature, or unique identification feature.

Section 66D

Cheating by personation using a computer resource — pretending to be someone else to deceive or defraud.

Section 66E

Violation of privacy — capturing, publishing, or transmitting images that violate a person's privacy without consent.

⚠️ This section provides general awareness information and is not legal advice. Laws and their application can change — refer to the official legislation for the current text at India Code, the Government of India's legislation portal.
