What is Cybercrime?

"It doesn't start with a computer.
It starts with a person."

TRUST
→
DECEPTION
→
ACCESS
→
DAMAGE

Cybercrime is illegal activity carried out using computers, mobile devices, networks or the internet. It can involve stealing information, money, accounts or identities.

Example: You get a text that looks like it's from your bank (Trust). It says your account will be blocked unless you verify now (Deception). You click the link and enter your login details (Access). Within minutes, your account is drained (Damage).

🎭 SECTION 02

Common Cybercrimes

🎣

Phishing

Fake links
Tap for details

How it works: Fake emails or websites impersonate trusted brands to steal your login details.

What they want: Passwords, OTPs, account access.

Warning signs: Unknown sender, urgent tone, mismatched URL.

Stay safe: Never click unverified links — go to the site directly instead.

📱

SMS Scam

Fake alerts
Tap for details

How it works: A text pretends to be your bank/courier, pushing you to click a link.

What they want: Personal or financial info via a fake landing page.

Warning signs: "Account blocked", "Update KYC now", shortened links.

Stay safe: Verify through the official app, not the link in the text.

💳

Bank Fraud

Payment scam
Tap for details

How it works: Fake payment requests, QR codes, or "refund" calls trick you into sending money.

What they want: Your UPI PIN, card details, or a direct transfer.

Warning signs: Being asked to enter a PIN to "receive" money.

Stay safe: Receiving money never needs a PIN. Ever.

🛒

Shopping Scam

Fake stores
Tap for details

How it works: Fake stores offer huge discounts, take payment, and never ship anything.

What they want: Your card details and upfront payment.

Warning signs: Unrealistic prices, no contact info, brand-new site.

Stay safe: Check reviews and site age before you buy.

📞

Vishing

Fake calls
Tap for details

How it works: A caller poses as a bank employee or official to build trust over the phone.

What they want: OTP, PIN, CVV — spoken directly to them.

Warning signs: Pressure to act immediately, requests for codes.

Stay safe: No real bank ever asks for your OTP or PIN on a call.

👤

Identity Theft

Data theft
Tap for details

How it works: Stolen personal data is used to open accounts or impersonate you.

What they want: Your name, ID numbers, and personal details.

Warning signs: Unfamiliar accounts, unexpected bills or notices.

Stay safe: Limit what personal info you share publicly online.

⚖️ SECTION 03

Know Your Cyber Law

⚖️ CYBER LAW FILE

IT ACT, 2000

India's primary legislation addressing electronic activity and cyber offences.

Section 43

Deals with certain unauthorized acts involving computer systems, such as accessing or damaging a system without permission.

Section 66

Covers certain computer-related offences, including hacking-related conduct, when the conditions of the provision are met.

Section 66C / 66D

Relate to identity theft and cheating by impersonation using computer resources.

⚠️ Important: Cyber laws can be amended and their application depends on the facts of a case. This page is for awareness, not legal advice.

EMERGENCY HELP →

If money has been lost or a payment was made:

  • Contact your bank/payment provider immediately using its official app, website, or the number on your card.
  • For cyber financial fraud in India, call 1930 immediately.
  • Report the incident on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, and messages as evidence.
