Know the Enemy 🕵️

Scammers have a whole playbook — here are the 5 tricks they run most often. Tap a card to flip it and see how each one really works.

🎣

Phishing

The classic bait-and-hook 👆 Tap to reveal

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites dressed up to look legit — all designed to get you to hand over passwords, OTPs, or banking details.

Spot the hook:

  • Sender you don't recognize
  • "Do this NOW or else" urgency
  • A link that feels... off
  • Asking for your password or OTP
📱

Smishing

SMS + Phishing 👆 Tap to reveal

📱 Smishing

Same trick as phishing, delivered straight to your texts. One tap on the wrong link and your info is gone.

"Your bank account will be blocked. Click here to update your KYC."

Real banks don't threaten you into clicking random links — screenshot it, don't tap it.

📞

Vishing

Voice + Phishing 👆 Tap to reveal

📞 Vishing

A calm, confident voice on the phone claiming to be your bank, a government office, or "support" — built entirely to earn your trust fast.

⚠️ Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV, or password on a call — no matter who they say they are.
🛒

Shopping Scams

Too good to be true 👆 Tap to reveal

🛒 Online Shopping Scams

Fake stores pop up overnight, dangle unbeatable prices, take your payment — and the "package" never comes.

  • Prices that seem impossible
  • No real contact info anywhere
  • Weird or unofficial payment methods
  • Site looks brand new / no reviews
💳

Banking & Payment Fraud

Where the real money moves 👆 Tap to reveal

💳 Banking & Payment Fraud

Fake calls, texts, QR codes, or "payment requests" — all engineered to get you to send money or reveal financial details yourself.

💡 Remember: receiving money never requires entering your UPI PIN. If an app asks you to enter your PIN to "receive" a payment — that's the scam.

🛡️ 10 Golden Rules for Online Safety

Print these in your brain. Seriously.

1
🔐 Never share your OTP or PIN — with anyone, ever.
2
🔗 Don't blindly click links, even from people you know.
3
🌐 Check the website address carefully before typing anything.
4
📞 Don't trust unexpected calls claiming to be from your bank.
5
💰 "Easy money" offers are never actually easy — or real.
6
📧 Double-check the sender's actual email address, not just the name.
7
🔑 Use strong, unique passwords — not "123456" for everything.
8
🔒 Turn on 2-factor authentication wherever it's offered.
9
📲 Keep your phone and apps updated — patches close real gaps.
10
🚨 See something suspicious? Report it — don't just ignore it.
