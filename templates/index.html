What is Cyber Fraud?

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords, bank details, or personal data by pretending to be a trusted source.

Identity Theft

Criminals use stolen personal information to open accounts, make purchases, or commit fraud in your name, often causing long-term financial damage.

Fake Websites

Scammers clone legitimate websites (banks, shopping sites) with slightly altered URLs to capture login credentials and payment details.

How to Stay Safe

Never click unknown links, verify sender addresses, look for HTTPS and correct domain spelling, and enable two-factor authentication everywhere.
